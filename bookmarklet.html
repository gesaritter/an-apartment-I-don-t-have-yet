Two buttons for your bookmarks bar. One grabs the single listing you're looking at; the other reads your whole saved-items page in one go. Both work by reading the page in your own browser, so nothing gets blocked.

One at a time · Where does your site live?

Paste the address of your published site (the Vercel or GitHub Pages link). This lets the button open your add form directly. Leave it empty if you haven't published yet — the button will hand you a copy-paste snippet instead.

✛ Add to Finds

☝︎ drag this button up to your bookmarks bar — don't click it here

Mode: copy-paste snippet (no site address set)

The other one · Grab a whole list

This is the big time-saver. Drag this second button up to your bookmarks bar too. Click it while you're on your Merkliste or Vinted favourites page and it reads every saved item on the page at once — no site address needed, and nothing gets blocked because it's reading the page in your own browser.

⛏ Grab whole list

☝︎ drag this one up to your bookmarks bar as well

Scroll to the bottom of your list first — it can only see items the page has actually loaded.

Using them

Your whole list at once

  1. Show your bookmarks bar if it's hidden (Ctrl+Shift+B on Windows, Cmd+Shift+B on Mac) and drag both buttons above onto it.
  2. Open your Merkliste (kleinanzeigen.de/m-merkliste.html) or Vinted favourites (vinted.de/member/items/favourite_list).
  3. Scroll all the way to the bottom so every saved item actually loads onto the page.
  4. Click ⛏ Grab whole list. A box appears showing everything it found.
  5. Hit Copy all, then paste it into the "Import a whole list" box on your add page — or straight into data.js if you don't want to add notes.

A single listing

  1. Open one listing page.
  2. Click ✛ Add to Finds.
  3. Your add form opens with the title, link, photo, and price filled in — write your note, add tags, hit add.
Titles, prices, and photos are best-effort guesses read off the page layout, so give them a glance before saving. If either site redesigns its pages the grabber may start missing things — that's fixable, it just needs the selectors updated.
